Build a complete, highly polished, interactive AI career-readiness web application called:

SKILLGAP AI

TAGLINE:
“Know. Learn. Build. Prove. Get Ready.”

CORE CONCEPT:
SkillGap AI is an AI-powered Opportunity Readiness Platform for students.

The visual identity of the entire website should represent a transformation:

A confused/unprepared student enters the SkillGap AI portal →
their profile, resume, GitHub and projects are analyzed →
Skill gaps and evidence gaps are identified →
they receive personalized learning resources →
they practice →
build projects →
prove their skills →
reassess →
and finally walk out of the portal as a more confident, opportunity-ready candidate.

The website must feel like a premium AI startup product, not a generic resume analyzer.

Portal opens into bright environment.
